# Model kit

3D models from Kenney's **Nature Kit** and **Furniture Kit**.

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit and https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain). Attribution is not required;
  it is here because it is the right thing to do.

Only the models actually used are committed, in GLB form. Everything else in
the original packs — other formats, unused pieces — is left out.
